Privacy Policy — AliExpress Hunters

Last updated: 9 September 2026

AliExpress Hunters ("we", "the app") is an internal content tool operated by an individual creator to prepare and publish short-form product content to the operator's own social accounts. This policy explains what data the app handles when it connects to TikTok.

Information we access

  • TikTok account identifiers obtained through TikTok Login: your open ID and basic profile fields (display name, avatar URL) as granted by the user.info.basic scope.
  • OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) issued by TikTok under the video.upload scope, used to upload content to your own TikTok account.
  • Content you approve inside the tool — product images and the caption text — which are uploaded to TikTok as a draft for you to review and publish.

The app does not collect data about any other person, does not use tracking cookies, and does not run any advertising or analytics SDK.

How we use it

  • To authenticate you with TikTok and keep the session valid (token refresh).
  • To send content you have explicitly approved to your TikTok account as an unpublished draft via the TikTok Content Posting API.

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties for their own purposes.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored only on the operator's local machine, in a file that is not committed to version control and not transmitted anywhere except to TikTok's own API endpoints. Tokens are kept until they expire or until access is revoked, then deleted. Approved slide images are placed on a static hosting site (GitHub Pages) solely so that TikTok can fetch them by URL during upload; they are removed after upload.

Your choices

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and websites. Revoking immediately stops all further use.
  • To request deletion of any stored data, email the address below.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under the age required by TikTok's own Terms of Service in their region.
