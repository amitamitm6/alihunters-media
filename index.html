AliExpress Hunters

A private content tool used by a single creator to prepare short-form product carousels and upload them, after manual approval, as drafts to the operator's own TikTok account via the TikTok Content Posting API. Nothing is published automatically — the operator adds a sound and publishes each post inside the TikTok app.

The companion Telegram community is t.me/Aliexpress_Hunters.
